01 — About

Security-first, end to end

I'm Owen Levi Ezinkwo, a cybersecurity engineer and developer studying BSc Cybersecurity at the Air Force Institute of Technology (expected 2028). For the past three years I've worked freelance with businesses and individuals who needed their systems checked, hardened, or rebuilt properly.

My work sits in an unusual spot: I assess systems as an attacker would, then implement the fixes as an engineer would. That means vulnerability assessments and reporting on one project, and a hardened private cloud deployment or a secure web application on the next.

Python is my strongest language, and I use it for tooling, automation, and backend services. I build frontends with React and JavaScript, work in Java, and write Solidity when a project involves smart contracts. I hold certifications in Cybersecurity and in Cloud Computing / DevOps.

I care about systems that hold up under scrutiny — clear audit trails, proper access control, and infrastructure that doesn't fall over the first time someone pushes on it.
